WELCOME TO THE PUZZLE ROOM

A little orientation.

Restore all six faces to a single color. Each face turns a real outer layer of the cube, including the neighboring stickers.

Explore
Drag with a mouse or finger. Arrow keys orbit; Home restores the starting view.
Turn
U up, R right, F front, D down, L left, B back. Letters on the centers identify the fixed faces, even after orbiting.
Change direction
A plain letter turns clockwise, viewed directly at that face. Hold Shift for inverse (R′), or Alt for a double turn (R2). The direction selector also applies to letter keys.
Recover
Z undoes the latest turn. Fast taps queue in order. Restart returns to the same scramble; Reset solved gives a clean cube.
Challenge
New scramble applies 25 legal random turns. Inspect freely: the timer starts with your first turn and stops on completion. Undo marks an assisted session.

This is a practice room, with random-move scrambles rather than official competition random-state scrambles. No automatic solver is included. Sessions clear on page reload.
