# Chromatic puzzle room

A practice room for a six-color, three-layer twisty cube.

## Language

**Face**: One of six fixed outer surfaces, named Up, Right, Front, Down, Left and Back by its center. Orbiting the view does not rename faces.

**Turn**: One clockwise, inverse or double rotation of an outer layer, as seen from outside that face. A double turn counts as one turn.

**Cubie**: A physical corner, edge or center piece carrying its original set of stickers.

**Scramble**: A sequence of legal face turns that prepares a challenge from the solved cube.

**Baseline**: The scrambled cube to which Restart returns.

**Move trail**: The player turns still in effect after undo operations.

**Harmony**: The solved state, with every face a single color.
